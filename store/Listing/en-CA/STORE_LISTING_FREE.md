### Short description

### Full description

WinQuickSwitch - Free puts essential Windows controls in one fast, keyboard-friendly utility. Switch projection modes, choose audio endpoints, adjust individual app volumes, check connected devices, manage supported wireless radios, and control taskbar behaviour without hunting through multiple Settings pages.

Choose PC screen only, Duplicate, Extend, or Second screen only while preserving Windows-managed monitor placement and rotation. WinQuickSwitch detects the active projection mode and provides quick access to display status and Windows display settings.

Manage playback and microphone endpoints, Default and Communications roles, favourite devices, per-application volume, and mute controls. View connected or present Bluetooth and wired devices, check Wi-Fi and Bluetooth state, and open the relevant Windows settings when more detailed management is needed.

Use the standard app window, standalone floating controls, or the optional dimmed workspace overlay. Assign keyboard shortcuts to the app, feature panels, display modes, and favourite audio endpoints. Light and dark themes, startup behaviour, notification-area controls, and taskbar actions are included.

WinQuickSwitch - Free contains the complete core switching experience. Reusable Profiles and combined display/audio/device setups are available only in the paid edition.

The app works locally on your PC. It does not require an account and contains no advertising, analytics, telemetry, tracking software, or publisher-operated cloud service.

### What’s new in version 1.0.33.0

Initial Free release with display switching, audio endpoint controls, per-app volume and mute, device and wireless status, keyboard shortcuts, taskbar controls, floating controls, and the optional dimmed workspace overlay.

### WinQuickSwitch - Free Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 26, 2026  
Publisher: Andrew Chang  
Contact: andrew.chang0@outlook.com

WinQuickSwitch - Free is a local Windows utility. It does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, telemetry, tracking software, or publisher-operated cloud services.

To provide its features, the app reads Windows information such as display configuration, audio endpoint and active application-audio details, connected Bluetooth and wired-device names and status, wireless radio state, taskbar state, and the shortcuts, favourites, theme, startup, and interface preferences you configure.

Settings are stored locally for the current Windows user under `%LOCALAPPDATA%\WinQuickSwitch Free\`. The Free edition does not create or store Profiles. Some device, application, or favourite names may be identifying if you choose identifying names.

WinQuickSwitch - Free does not transmit this information to the publisher and does not sell, rent, or use it for advertising or profiling. Your data remains on your device unless you or Windows copy, back up, synchronize, or share it. You can remove local data by deleting the app’s settings folder or uninstalling the app.

If you enable startup, the app creates a startup entry for the current Windows user. You can remove it from within the app or Windows Settings.

Microsoft may independently process Store account, purchase, installation, diagnostic, or update information under Microsoft’s own terms and privacy statement.

For privacy questions, contact andrew.chang0@outlook.com.
