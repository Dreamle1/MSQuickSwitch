# WinQuickSwitch - Free Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 26, 2026  
Publisher: Andrew Chang  
Contact: andrew.chang0@outlook.com

WinQuickSwitch - Free is a local Windows utility. It does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, telemetry, tracking software, or publisher-operated cloud services.

To provide its features, the app reads Windows information such as display configuration, audio endpoint and active application-audio details, connected Bluetooth and wired-device names and status, wireless radio state, taskbar state, and the shortcuts, favourites, theme, startup, and interface preferences you configure.

Settings are stored locally for the current Windows user under `%LOCALAPPDATA%\WinQuickSwitch Free\`. The Free edition does not create or store Profiles. Some device, application, or favourite names may be identifying if you choose identifying names.

WinQuickSwitch - Free does not transmit this information to the publisher and does not sell, rent, or use it for advertising or profiling. Your data remains on your device unless you or Windows copy, back up, synchronize, or share it. You can remove local data by deleting the app’s settings folder or uninstalling the app.

If you enable startup, the app creates a startup entry for the current Windows user. You can remove it from within the app or Windows Settings.

Microsoft may independently process Store account, purchase, installation, diagnostic, or update information under Microsoft’s own terms and privacy statement.
